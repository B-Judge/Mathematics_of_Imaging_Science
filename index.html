BME 5700 · Study companion

Mathematics of Imaging Science

Interactive walkthroughs for the mathematics behind imaging systems: vector spaces and operators, delta functions, Fourier analysis, and how they come together to describe real imaging systems.

Each page pairs the formulas with pictures you can move, notation decoders, and worked examples. They are meant to be read alongside the textbook, not instead of it.

Based on
Harrison H. Barrett & Kyle J. Myers, Foundations of Image Science (Wiley, 2004). Section numbers in these walkthroughs (e.g. §3.3, §7.2) refer to that book.
