    Unwind, rewind

    Forward (−) unwinds: a clockwise coil cancels a component's spin and leaves a rod \(Ae^{i\varphi}\). Inverse (+) rewinds: spin that rod back up. \(e^{-i\theta}e^{+i\theta}=1\).

    Signal decomposition

    \[f(x)=c_0+\sum_n A_n\cos(2\pi\xi_nx+\varphi_n)\]

    Sweep the coil. Each wave unwinds into a rod at its own \(\xi_n\): length gives \(A_n\), direction gives \(\varphi_n\). Everything else cancels.

    sinc → rect: exact, if you sum everything

    The rect's corners live in the sinc's slow \(1/\xi\) tails. Cut the tails off and the corners ring. The ring narrows as \(R\) grows, but the 9% overshoot stays (Gibbs).
